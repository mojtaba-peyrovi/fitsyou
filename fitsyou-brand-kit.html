The wordmark
"fits" is always upright. "you" is always italic. The pink accent on "you" is the core brand signal — it communicates the product promise in the logo itself. Always lowercase, never title case or all-caps.

Usage rules
What to do and what not to do.
Do
fitsyou
Always lowercase. "fits" upright, "you" italic + pink.
Don't
fitsyou
Never all one colour. The two-tone split is the brand.
Don't
FitsYou
Never title case or caps. Reads as a proper noun, not a brand.
Don't
FITSYOU
Never all-caps. Destroys the serif character and the italic signal.
Do
fitsyou
Icon + wordmark lockup. Icon always to the left.
Don't
fitsyou
Never mix icon variants — pink icon with on-light wordmark clashes.
